Master Data Formatting Guide

The Golden Rule: Never modify, rename, add, or delete the first row (Header Row) of your CSV template. The Muziris ledger requires exact column alignment to securely process your catalog.

1. General Upload Rules

  • Grouping Variants: Use the Item Group Number column to group variations together. If a t-shirt comes in 3 sizes, type "1" in that column for all three rows. For your next unique product, type "2".
  • No Currency Symbols: Type prices as raw numbers (e.g., 12.50). Do not use dollar signs or euro symbols. Your currency is automatically determined by your Supplier ID.
  • No Commas in Numbers: Write quantities as 1500, not 1,500. (Commas break CSV code).
  • Image Linking: Because high-res images cannot fit inside a spreadsheet, simply type the exact file name (e.g., front-view.jpg) into the CSV image columns. Then, drag and drop the actual image file into Step 3 of the Staging Portal. The system will link them automatically.

2. Acceptable Values Explorer

To prevent database errors, your text must match our exact system categories. Select an option and click "Copy" to paste it perfectly into your CSV.
