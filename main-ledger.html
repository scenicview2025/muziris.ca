How we filter out trading companies and verify true manufacturers.

Legal & Financial KYC

We verify corporate business licenses, export permits, and tax records. We ensure you are dealing directly with the legal manufacturing entity, strictly cutting out unauthorized middlemen.

Physical Facility Audits

Factories must prove their production capabilities. We check equipment logs, square footage, workforce size, and international certifications (ISO, CE, FDA) to ensure they can scale with your demands.
