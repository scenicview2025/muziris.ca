The journey

From idea to inventory.

We've built a seamless supply chain tailored for Canadian small and medium businesses. Here's how your product moves.

Stage 1 · Sourcing & Sampling

We find the right factory

Tell us what you need. We match your specs to a verified Indian manufacturer, negotiate MOQs that work for SMEs, and manage sample production until it's perfect.

Factory vetting already done.
Stage 2 · Production & QC

We inspect every run

We monitor production and conduct pre-shipment inspections against your approved sample. Issues are caught and resolved before the goods ever leave the country.

Quality assured at the source.
Stage 3 · Logistics

We clear the borders

Air or ocean, we book the freight. We handle all export documentation from India and manage Canadian import clearance, keeping your goods moving smoothly.

No customs headaches.
Stage 4 · Delivery

We deliver to your door

Final-mile delivery straight to your Canadian warehouse, retail shop, or directly to an Amazon FBA prep center. All with a landed cost confirmed upfront.

Ready to sell.
