The journey

Four stages. One story, start to finish.

Here's exactly what happens to your product between the moment we agree on a spec and the moment it lands at your door.

Stage 1 · India

We source it

It starts on a factory floor in India. We match your spec to a verified manufacturer, negotiate MOQ and pricing directly at the source, and get you a physical sample before a single unit goes into production.

No factory relationship? We already have one.
Stage 2 · Inspection

We check it

Before anything leaves the factory, it's inspected against your specification — photos, video and, on request, an independent third-party inspector. Problems get caught here, not after they've crossed an ocean.

Verified before it ships, not after.
Stage 3 · In transit

We move it

We book the freight — ocean or air — prepare every export and import document, and manage clearance with Canadian customs so your shipment doesn't sit stuck at the border while paperwork catches up.

Duties and clearance, handled for you.
Stage 4 · Canada

We deliver it

Final-mile delivery to your warehouse, 3PL or Amazon FBA prep center — with landed cost confirmed in Canadian dollars before you ever committed to the order.

The number you saw is the number you pay.
