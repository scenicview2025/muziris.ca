Muziris Trade Assurance

We eliminate the risk of international trade. By securing the buyer's funds in our Canadian Escrow vault before you begin production, we guarantee that your hard work is always compensated.

Step 01

Buyer Secures Funds

When an order is agreed upon, the international buyer deposits the funds into the secure Muziris Escrow Account. We verify the funds are fully cleared and locked.

Step 02

Official Purchase Order

Muziris issues you a legally binding Purchase Order confirming the funds are secured.

Need Working Capital? You can present this guaranteed PO to your local bank or financial partners to easily secure short-term manufacturing loans.

Step 03

Production & Shipping

With guaranteed payment waiting for you, you can confidently purchase raw materials, manufacture the goods, and ship the order to the buyer without fear of cancellation or fraud.

Step 04

Delivery & Inspection

The buyer receives the shipment and has a strict 7-day window to inspect the goods. They must verify that the quality matches the agreed-upon standards.

Step 05

Guaranteed Payout

Once the buyer approves the goods (or if 7 days pass with no dispute raised), the funds are automatically released from our vault directly to your registered bank account, minus our standard platform fee.
