1. NON-CIRCUMVENTION & ANTI-BYPASS
By submitting this quotation, the Supplier legally agrees to strict non-circumvention. The Supplier shall not attempt to identify, contact, or transact with the Buyer off-platform. 2. ESCROW PAYMENT TIERS
Muziris operates a secure milestone-based escrow system to protect both parties.
  • Tier 1 (Deals 1-3): 100% of the funds are secured in the Muziris Vault prior to production. Funds are released to the Supplier exactly 7 days after the Buyer receives the goods and completes inspection.
  • Tier 2 (Deals 4+): Standard terms unlocked. 30% Advance paid at the time of shipment; 70% Balance paid 7 days post-delivery.
