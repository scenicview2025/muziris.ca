The Supplier Journey

From local production to global fulfillment. Follow the golden thread of the Muziris network.

01. Identity

Seamless Registration

Once approved, you receive a secure, permanent Supplier ID. No complex passwords—just fast, secure verification.

02. Ledger

Frictionless Uploads

Use our Bulk CSV Staging Portal. Our system organizes your product matrix instantly and optimizes images.

03. Assurance

Secure Escrow

Funds are deposited into the Muziris Canadian Escrow vault. We issue a Purchase Order you can use for bank financing.

04. Logistics

Global Inspection

Ship with confidence. Upon arrival, the buyer has a 7-day window to inspect goods against specs.

05. Payout

Instant Settlement

Once approved, funds are released from Escrow directly to your bank account, minus transparent fees.
