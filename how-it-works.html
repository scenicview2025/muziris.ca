The Supplier Journey

We built Muziris to be frictionless and secure. From uploading your massive product catalogs to guaranteeing your payment across borders, here is exactly how our platform works for you.

01. Identity & Access

Seamless Registration

Apply to the network with your company details. Once approved by our team, you receive a secure, permanent Supplier ID and a unique Login PIN. No complex passwords to forget—just fast, secure verification.

02. The Global Ledger

Frictionless Catalog Uploads

Have 5 products? Use our clean Single-Upload portal. Have 500 products? Use our Bulk CSV Staging Portal. Our system automatically catches formatting errors, resizes your heavy images, and organizes your matrix instantly.

03. Trade Assurance

Secure Orders & PO Financing

When a buyer agrees to your terms, they deposit the funds into the secure Muziris Canadian Escrow vault. We issue you a legally binding Purchase Order, which you can use to secure raw material loans from your local bank.

04. Logistics & Quality

Production & Inspection

Manufacture and ship the goods with 100% confidence that the money is already locked in our vault. Upon arrival, the buyer has a strict 7-day window to inspect the goods against your provided specifications.

05. Completion

Guaranteed Payout

Once the buyer approves the shipment (or if the 7 days pass without a dispute), the funds are instantly released from Escrow directly to your registered bank account, minus our standard transparent platform fee.

Enterprise Data Support

We know that structuring large amounts of product data can be difficult. If you are struggling to format your CSV files or map your existing database to the Muziris Ledger, our dedicated Data Team will do it for you. Just send us your raw Excel files, and we will prepare them for perfect upload.
