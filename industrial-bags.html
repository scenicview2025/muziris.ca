Bulk bags, sacks and packing pouches

FIBC means Flexible Intermediate Bulk Container, commonly called a bulk bag or jumbo bag. U-panel describes the bag body; baffles are internal corner panels; ventilation comes from open fabric sections. These features can be combined.
