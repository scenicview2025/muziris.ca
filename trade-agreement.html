Muziris Escrow & Governance Terms

1. Role of Muziris International: Muziris acts strictly as an independent escrow agent and compliance facilitator. We do not own the inventory. By signing this agreement, both parties consent to Muziris holding the total contract value in a neutral trust account until delivery conditions are met.

2. The 7-Day Payment Release: Funds shall remain locked in the Muziris Vault while goods are in transit. Upon confirmed arrival at the destination port/facility, the Buyer has exactly seven (7) calendar days to inspect the goods. If no formal dispute is logged within this window, funds are automatically and irreversibly released to the Supplier.

3. Logistics & Shipping Variance: Shipping rates provided at checkout are estimates based on Supplier dimensions. Because global freight markets fluctuate, the Buyer is responsible for any verifiable shipping cost increases exceeding 10% of the original estimate prior to final dispatch.

4. Foreign Exchange (FX) Handling: The Supplier assumes all exchange rate risks once this contract is locked. The Buyer shall fund the escrow in the designated platform currency (e.g., CAD/USD). The Supplier's final payout will be calculated based on the exchange rate captured at the exact moment this agreement is digitally signed.
