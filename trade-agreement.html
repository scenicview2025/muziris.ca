Governance & Tazapay Escrow Terms

1. Role of Muziris & Contractual Parties: Muziris acts strictly as a Marketplace Facilitator and technology provider. This agreement forms a binding commercial contract directly between the Buyer and the Supplier. All financial transactions and escrow holds are processed strictly by Tazapay, a licensed global payment institution. Muziris does not hold funds and assumes zero liability for manufacturing defects or delivery delays.

2. The 7-Day Payment Release: Funds shall remain securely locked in a Tazapay-managed Escrow Account while goods are in transit. Upon confirmed delivery to the destination, the Buyer has exactly seven (7) calendar days to inspect the goods. If no formal dispute is logged within this window, funds are automatically and irreversibly released to the Supplier.

3. Logistics & Shipping Variance: Shipping rates provided at checkout are active market estimates. Because global freight markets fluctuate, the Buyer is responsible for any verifiable shipping cost increases exceeding 10% of the original estimate prior to final dispatch.

4. Foreign Exchange & Refund Policy: To ensure global stability, the Escrow vault is strictly anchored and held in US Dollars (USD). The Buyer will fund the escrow using their local currency (CAD) via Tazapay's live exchange rate at checkout. In the rare event of a cancellation or dispute resulting in a refund, the exact USD amount held in the vault will be returned. Muziris and Tazapay are not liable for standard foreign exchange fluctuations or bank conversion fees that occur during the refund process.

5. Third-Party Payment Services (Tazapay): Payment processing and escrow services are provided by Tazapay. By digitally signing this agreement and funding the escrow, the Buyer agrees to be bound by Tazapay’s Terms of Service and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) policies.
