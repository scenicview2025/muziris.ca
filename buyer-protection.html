The Future of
Risk-Free Sourcing

We eliminate the risk of international wire transfers. With the Muziris Vault, your capital never reaches the factory until your cargo clears inspection.

1. Connect & Negotiate

Browse the Muziris global catalog and negotiate directly with Tier-1 manufacturers through our secure chat interface. Once you agree on volume, customizations, and pricing, the factory will issue an Official RFQ (Request for Quotation).

Vetted Manufacturers Only

Every factory in our network has undergone strict legal, financial, and capacity verification. No middlemen. No trading companies. Trade with the source.

2. The Muziris Escrow Vault

When you accept the quote, you do not send money overseas to a foreign bank account. Instead, your funds are deposited into the North American Muziris Escrow Vault. The factory is notified that the funds are secured, giving them the guarantee needed to begin production.

3. Production & Managed Logistics

The factory manufactures your order to spec. Once completed, Muziris integrated logistics partners handle the factory pickup, port customs compliance, and ocean/air freight routing. You track the shipment live via your buyer dashboard.

4. The 7-Day Inspection Hold

Your cargo arrives at your destination. The clock starts now. You have 7 full days to inspect the goods, verify the quality, and ensure the order perfectly matches the negotiated contract. During this time, your funds remain firmly locked in the Muziris Vault.

Total Buyer Leverage

If the goods are defective or incorrect, the funds are frozen, and Muziris steps in to mediate the dispute or issue a refund.

5. Final Payout Release

Only after you click "Approve Cargo" in your dashboard (or after the 7-day window expires without a dispute), does the smart contract execute. The Muziris Vault finally wires the funds to the overseas factory.
