The New Standard in B2B Trade

We eliminated the risk of international sourcing by placing a bank-grade escrow vault between enterprise buyers and global factories.

1. Discover & Negotiate

Browse our global ledger of verified factories. Submit an RFQ to negotiate volume pricing, custom packaging, and private labeling.

2. Fund the Escrow

Review the official factory quote and fund the contract. Your money is locked safely in the Muziris Vault while the factory begins production.

3. Inspect & Release

The goods are shipped via our integrated logistics network. Funds are only released to the factory 7 days after you successfully receive the order.
